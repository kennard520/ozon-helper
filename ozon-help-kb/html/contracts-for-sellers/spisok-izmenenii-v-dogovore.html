01.10.2026

(publication date 23.09.2026)

From 01 October 2026: 

  • The operating and payment procedures for Premium have been clarified (Clause 2, Section "Additional Services"). 

  • Clause 2.2.12 of Article 2 "LOGISTICS PARTNER (AGGREGATOR)", Section "Sales from the Seller's Warehouse", has been added: a claim regarding a Shipment may be submitted to Ozon Support within 30 days if the Shipment has not been returned to the Warehouse, the Seller's Warehouse, or the Seller's Partner's Warehouse within 60 days. 

Contract for the placement of goods from abroad on Ozon Platform (cross-border) as of 01.10.2026 (PDF)

25.09.2026

(publication date 17.09.2026)

From 21 September 2026:

  • It is prohibited to recreate a product description page with the same brand or content. A product may only be republished if the dispute with the copyright holder has already been resolved and no rights have been infringed (clause 2, Section “Product Description Page”).

  • Section “Definitions”; clause 1.2 of the Section “Subject, Conclusion, Amendment and Termination of the Contract”; clause 6 of the Section “Miscellaneous Provisions” have been amended.

  • The terms of use and the list of services of generation and processing of materials have been updated (clause 1.2, Section “Product Description Page”).

From 25 September 2026:

  • The minimum period to add products to a promotion before it starts has been changed from 3 to 5 business days. (clause 3.10, Section "Promotion services for Seller's goods and advertising services").

  • The list of reasons for the Product Disposal has been added: the product was not sold in accordance with the established number of re-sale attempts upon return through the Warehouse (clause 8.2, Section “Return, Removal and Disposal”).

  • Provisions on the liability of the Seller and Ozon have been supplemented (Section "Liability").

Contract for the placement of goods from abroad on Ozon Platform (cross-border) as of 25.09.2026 (PDF)

24.08.2026

(publication date 14.08.2026)

  • Provisions on the liability of the Seller and Ozon have been supplemented (Section "Liability"), in particular:

    • The procedure for determining the amount of compensation depending on the sales scheme has been updated in Article 5.

    • Ozon has the right to refuse to pay compensation if the barcode (for example, EAN or OZN), which is pasted on the product, was not indicated in the product description page when the Shipment was transferred.

  • The "Sales from the Seller's Warehouse" Section has been supplemented with the "OGL" subsection, which provides for a new sales scheme from the Seller's warehouse:

    • The procedure for interaction between the parties regarding OGL sales has been established — the subject matter and cost of services, transfer and acceptance of Shipments, and the compensation procedure (Section "Sales from the Seller's Warehouse").

  • The moment of fulfillment of obligations by Ozon has been adjusted (clause 2.1.3, Section "Logistics Partner (Aggregator)").

  • Ozon's rights and obligations under the agreement with Logistics Partner with respect to Shipments returned through the Warehouse shall not be transferred to the Seller (clause 7.4, Section "Return, Removal and Disposal").

23.07.2026
(publication date 15.07.2026)

  • The structure of the Contract has been updated:

    • Section "Sale from the Seller's Warehouse", which includes the subsections "rFBS" (previously the Section "Requirements for Order Processing") and "Logistics Partner (Aggregator)" (previously the Annex No. 2 "Procedure for the Provision of Services by Logistics Partners"), has been added;

    • Section "Return, Removal, and Disposal" (previously the Article 5 of Section " Requirements for Order Processing") has been added;

    • Section "Sale from the Seller's Partner's Warehouse" (previously the Section "Partners for Services") has been added;

    • Section "Miscellaneous Provisions" (previously the paragraphs 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, 1.12, 1.13, 2.5, 2.6, 2.7, 4.9, 4.10) has been added;

    • the Annex No. 1 "Terms of Sale by Residents of the Commonwealth of Independent States" has been removed.

  • The term "Customer" has been changed to "Seller" (Section "Definitions") in the Russian version of the Contract;

  • The terms "Ozon Forwarder" and "Logistics Partner" have been added (Section "Definitions");

  • Ozon has the right to unilaterally amend the Contract by sending the Seller a notice at least 1 (one) calendar day prior to the entry into force of any improvement of the Seller's position, including the granting of new rights, benefits, discounts, or expansion of the Platform's technical functionality, as well as editorial, technical, reference, or clarifying corrections (Clause 1.5, Section "Subject, Conclusion, Amendment and Termination of the Contract ");

  • The list of reporting documents for the Seller has been clarified (Clause 3.2.1, Section "Remuneration, Reporting Documents and Payment");

  • The provisions on the liability of the Seller and Ozon have been supplemented (Section "Liability"), in particular:

    • Article 4 "Allocation of Entrepreneurial Risk" has been added;

    • Article 4 " Responsibility of the Parties" of Annex No. 2 "Procedure for the Provision of Services by Logistics Partners" has been moved to the Section "Liability";

    • The procedure for determining the amount of compensation depending on the sales scheme or in the event of a product return is specified in the Article 5.

  • Ozon has the right to collect a fine from the Seller if Ozon discovers violations of the product description page content requirements in the Product Description Page (Clause 4, Section "Product Description Page").
